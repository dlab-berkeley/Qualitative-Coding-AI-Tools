You knew you was getting so many warm bodies. You didn't know anything about them. And they had a number, some Social Security number, or transport number, and you put this down on their pay book, and that's what you went by. And then we'd give them an individual field number: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and so on.

We kept a log, or weight book, all week of what we picked, and everybody's name, if it was our kind of people, Caucasian, we had their name up there, and the Hispanics, we had their numbers. And we kept their weights and numbers by the day. Then we transferred them to these logbooks at the end of the week. My sister helped him with the first group. But she got married and left. So the '59 group, I had to move up and start doing — start filling the books out.
