I remember it very well because it was very – oh, I guess I was younger. I think I was probably eight, something like that, when we first got – I remember, I think, we had 19 the first year, and oh, I remember anticipating them coming and them preparing the house for them to live in. I remember them going over into Texas to pick them up. His name was Tony, and he was our interpreter. He was very, very nice. He had a young family in Mexico, and he wanted to stay on so bad. Mostly, he talked about his family. He wanted to bring them to the U.S., to have a better life for them than he could provide for them in Mexico.
