# Exercise 1 — Hand-Code the Passages

**Time: 16 minutes**

For this first exercise, **do not use AI**.

Open the [codebook](codebook.md) and work through the six short excerpts below.

Spend about **12 minutes working on your own**. Then spend the last **4 minutes comparing with one person near you**.

You are not trying to guess an answer key. Make the coding decision that seems most defensible to you.

For every code you assign:

1. write down the code;
2. copy the exact words that support it; and
3. give a short reason for your choice.

> **No quote, no code.**

More than one code can apply to the same passage. You can also decide that none of the codes fit.

## Before we discuss as a group

Hold on to the disagreements.

We are going to use them.

The point of this exercise is not to establish that human coding is perfectly consistent before we bring in AI. It isn't.

The question is what we can learn from **where** people disagree, **why** they disagree, and whether the codebook gives us enough guidance to make those decisions visible.
