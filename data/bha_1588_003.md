I think there was mixed feelings. I do remember some of the merchants – I know the merchants made – they made a lot of money from them, but I do remember some of them not being very nice to them. Dressed very much with new clothes. They were dressed very poorly when they came. I'm sure it was money orders. I know I remember them sending money back home a lot, and I'm pretty sure it's probably money orders.
